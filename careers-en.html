Internship Program

We believe early career experiences are transformational and a cornerstone of professional growth. We offer an internship program designed to support young professionals and foster the development of new talent in sustainability-focused careers.

Candidate profile:

  • Recent graduate or currently enrolled in the final year/semester of an undergraduate or graduate degree.
  • Fully bilingual (English and Spanish).
  • Proven professional interest in sustainable urban development and climate action.
  • Full-time commitment of at least 2 months.

What we offer:

  • Hands-on involvement in real national and international consulting projects.
  • Access to our specialized analytical platforms and methodologies.
  • Close mentorship and a collaborative work environment.
  • Monthly financial stipend (approx. $3,800 MXN / month).

How to apply: Send your resume and cover letters in both English and Spanish to ideas@capsus.mx.
